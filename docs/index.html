The nth member

REVOTT's numerator, working on its own. Every fixed Y generates a 400-node, 704-edge SFO over X. This page locates any instant on that SFO — what fires, which edges are open and how far along, the sentences on the instant's line — and walks the micronodes along every open edge: granules of the trajectory, judged against the member's own history, refined where deemed relevant.
